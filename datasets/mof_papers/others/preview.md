Is the emission intensity of the DMF-solvated europium(III) metal-organic framework {[Eu(L)(H₂O)₂]·DMF}ₙ in water stable over time during luminescence monitoring, where L is the deprotonated 2′-nitro[1,1′-biphenyl]-3,4′,5-tricarboxylate ligand?

**assistant**

## 3

**user**

What volumetric thermal expansion coefficients are obtained for the hydroxo-bridged indium biphenyl-3,3′,5,5′-tetracarboxylate framework upon heating?

**assistant**

The framework undergoes negative thermal expansion, with its unit-cell volume decreasing monotonically upon heating. Laboratory variable-temperature powder X-ray diffraction with Le Bail fitting gives αV = −6.91 × 10⁻⁶ K⁻¹, while high-resolution variable-temperature synchrotron powder X-ray diffraction with Rietveld refinement gives αV = −6.44 × 10⁻⁶ K⁻¹. The small difference may reflect variations in the heating and cooling equipment.

## 4

**user**

What crystal system, space group, Z, unit-cell parameters, volume, and formula sum describe single-crystal [NaCu(O3P-C2H4-SO3)(H2O)2]?

**assistant**

It is monoclinic, space group P21/c, with Z=4 and formula sum CuC2H8NaPSO8. The unit cell has a=14.757(3) Å, b=7.690(1) Å, c=7.481(2) Å, α=γ=90°, β=92.41(3)°, and V=848.3(3) Å^3.

What crystal system and space group are observed for the benzyl acetate-containing gadolinium(III) metal-organic framework?

It crystallizes in the centrosymmetric monoclinic system and in space group C2/c.
