# 当前 messages 中的实际语料预览

结构和光谱路径相对于 llm_corpus 根目录，不是相对于本文件。

## 1

**user**

Can you construct a different polymorph with the same composition as the given structure?

**assistant**

This is a different polymorph of the same material.

## 2

**user**

Can you produce another polymorph while preserving the composition of the given structure?

**assistant**

The output is an alternative polymorph of the given material.

## 3

**user**

Generate a different polymorph of the same composition, requiring its energy above the convex hull to be lower than that of the original structure.

**assistant**

Here is a different polymorph that keeps the composition unchanged and has a lower energy above the convex hull than that of the given structure.

## 4

**user**

Can you produce another polymorph while preserving the composition of the given structure?

**assistant**

The output structure represents a different polymorph with unchanged composition.

## 5

**user**

Keep the composition unchanged and produce a different polymorph.

**assistant**

Here is a different polymorph of the input material.

## 6

**user**

Generate a different polymorph of this material. Keep the composition unchanged and ensure that its energy above the convex hull is lower than that of the input structure.

**assistant**

The output structure has the same composition but is a different polymorph, with a lower energy above the convex hull than that of the input structure.
